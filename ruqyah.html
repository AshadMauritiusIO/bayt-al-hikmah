1. Understanding Unseen Afflictions & Physical Reactions

Ruqyah Dynamics: Spiritual Mechanisms vs. Physiological Responses

In orthodox Islamic theology, belief in the realm of the unseen (Al-Ghayb) is a fundamental pillar of faith. This realm encompasses angels, jinn, the evil eye ('Ayn / Hasad), and sorcery (Sihr). When the Quran is recited over an individual for spiritual treatment (Ruqyah Shar'iyyah), physical and involuntary body reactions frequently manifest.

Observations such as persistent coughing, throat clearing, uncomfortable tightness in the chest, burping, or feeling a lump in the throat represent the intersection of spiritual friction and human biology:

Spiritual Mechanisms ('Uqad & Exit)

Afflictions from magic or the evil eye often construct spiritual "knots" ('Uqad) residing in energy points such as the chest or throat. Divine recitation breaks these blockages, inducing throat discomfort, sudden urges to cough, or burping as the effect dissolves. Additionally, jinn resisting recitation may attempt to exit or manifest through the vocal cords.

Physiological & Somatic Factors

Deep listening and emotional focus stimulate the vagus nerve, which innervates the throat and gastrointestinal tract, causing swallowing reflexes, throat spasms, or acid reflux. Anxiety and somatic responses can also produce globus sensation (a feeling of a lump in the throat). Islam mandates ruling out organic medical conditions before concluding spiritual causes.

3. Authentic Prophetic Supplications (Adhkar)

Traditional Invocations for Daily Protection and Healing

Protection against All Harm Sahih Muslim (2708)
« أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ »
Transliteration: A'ūdhu bi-kalimāti-llāhi-t-tāmmāti min sharri mā khalaq.
English: "I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He has created."
Français : « Je cherche protection auprès des paroles parfaites d'Allah contre le mal de ce qu'Il a créé. »
Morning & Evening Shield Sunan Abi Dawud (5088) / Sunan At-Tirmidhi (3388)
« بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ »
Transliteration: Bismillāhi-lladhī lā yaḍurru ma'a-shmihi shay'un fī-l-arḍi wa lā fī-s-samā'i wa huwa-s-samī'u-l-'alīm.
English: "In the name of Allah, with Whose name nothing can cause harm on earth or in the heavens, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing."
Français : « Au nom d'Allah, tel qu'en compagnie de Son nom rien ne peut nuire sur terre ni dans le ciel, et Il est l'Audient, l'Omniscient. »

4. Step-by-Step Practical Self-Ruqyah Protocol

STEP 01: Preparation

Perform wudu (ablution), ensure clean surroundings, sit facing the Qiblah, and maintain absolute trust in Allah's healing power (Tawakkul).

STEP 02: Recitation & Blowing

Recite Surah Al-Fatiha, Ayat Al-Kursi, and the 3 Quls (Ikhlas, Falaq, Nas). Blow lightly with fine spittle (Nafth) into hands and wipe over the body, or into water/olive oil.

STEP 03: Application

Drink from the recited water, wash with it, or apply the recited olive oil over areas of pain or chest tightness twice daily.
