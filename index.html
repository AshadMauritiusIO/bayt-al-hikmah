The Identity Manifesto

بيان الهوية لبيت الحكمة

1. Mission & Preservation

Bayt Al-Hikmah (The Digital House of Wisdom) is dedicated to preserving, organizing, and transmitting classical Islamic scholarship and prophetic traditions using modern web standards while upholding absolute scholastic integrity.

2. Scholarly Authenticity (الضبط العلمي)

Every text, manuscript, and narration recorded in this platform is cross-referenced with traditional commentaries and verified chains of transmission (Isnād).

3. Universal Access

Knowledge in Bayt Al-Hikmah is maintained as a free, open-access public resource for scholars, students, and curious minds globally.
